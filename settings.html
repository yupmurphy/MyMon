What should MyMon call you?

Your username

Unlike your name above, a username belongs to one person only. It is how friends will find you once MyMon learns about friends.

@

Three to twenty characters. Letters, numbers and underscores, starting with a letter.

Currency

Every expense keeps the currency it was logged in. Changing this affects what you add from now on — 800 lei spent last week stay 800 lei, because that is what they were. A month holding two currencies shows two totals, side by side; MyMon never converts one into the other.

How you sign in

Google account —

Your email comes from Google and cannot be changed here. MyMon never sees, asks for or stores a password.

What rings the bell

The bell sits in the top corner of every page. These choices are kept with your account rather than in this browser, so they hold on every device you sign in on.

Somebody wants to be friends

Either half of it — a request arriving, and somebody you asked saying yes. One switch, because they are two ends of the same conversation and nobody wants half of it.

You are invited to a group

Somebody asks you to share a group with them. With this off the invitation still waits for you under Groups — you just will not be told the moment it arrives.

Somebody comments on your expense

One of your own entries in a shared group gets a reply. This is the one worth keeping on — it is usually a question about something you wrote down.

Any comment in your groups

Every reply in a group you are in, not only the ones about your own entries. The chattiest of the three, and the first to turn off if the bell starts ringing too often.

On your phone

Not yet. The switches above decide what reaches the bell; making a phone ring while MyMon is closed needs a piece that is not built — something on the server side to do the ringing, and on an iPhone, MyMon added to the home screen first. It is on the list, so if your phone stays quiet it is not a setting you have missed.
