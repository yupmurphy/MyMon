About MyMon

MyMon is a small personal expense tracker built for clarity, not for completeness. It answers one question well: what did this month cost me, and where did it go?

What it does

  • Logs an expense: amount, category, date and an optional comment.
  • Shows the total for a month, with the split by category in money and percent.
  • Compares the month you are looking at with the one before it.
  • Lets you step back through previous months, and charts the ones behind you.
  • Searches everything you ever logged, by note, amount or date.
  • Hands the lot over as a spreadsheet whenever you want it.
  • Shares a ledger with a few people, in a group, with comments on it.
  • Rings a bell when somebody invites you to a group or comments on an entry.

The rules

  • An amount is at least 0.01 and never negative.
  • A date is never in the future, and not more than 20 years back.
  • An expense can be corrected later, and a deletion can be undone.
  • Months are plain calendar months — no closing, no salary cycles.
  • The seven categories are fixed, and one of them is Other.

Not here, yet

  • Income, refunds and loans
  • Splitting a group total per person
  • A friends list, so inviting is not typing a username
  • A monthly limit, and expenses that repeat
  • Custom categories — see below

How groups work

A group is a few people and a ledger they all write into. A group expense is always a row of its own — never a window onto your personal list. Either you type it straight into the group, or you copy one of your own across, and from that moment the two are independent: changing one leaves the other alone.

This is why nothing of yours can land in a group by accident, and why the database never has to show anybody else your own expenses. Only the group's owner can invite or remove people, only the person who wrote an entry can change or delete it, and leaving a group does not take your entries out of it — if five people split a trip, the total still has to be the total.

Until you accept an invitation, all you can see of a group is its name.

Where your data lives

Your expenses are stored in a database, under your account, so you see the same numbers on your laptop and on your phone. Clearing your browser no longer costs you anything.

You sign in with your Google account. MyMon never sees, asks for or stores a password — Google confirms who you are, and that is all we get.

The database decides who may read what, not the app in your browser. Your own expenses are yours alone. What you put into a group is readable by the people in that group, and by nobody else — including your name, which other people only ever see once you share a group with them.

Light and dark

MyMon follows whatever your device is set to. The button in the corner overrules it, on that device, and remembers. The two themes do not share a palette: the seven category colours were chosen and checked separately for each, because a colour that separates cleanly on white does not on black. That is also why you cannot add your own categories — a colour nobody checked would walk straight into that set.
