Add MyMon to your home screen

Three taps in Safari, and it behaves like any other app.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. That is it.

It has to be Safari — other browsers on iPhone do not offer this.
